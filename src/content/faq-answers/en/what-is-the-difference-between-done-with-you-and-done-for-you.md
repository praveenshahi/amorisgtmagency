Both models run the same methodology and both ship in six weeks. What changes is who makes the infrastructure decisions.

**Done With You** deploys onto the stack you already have. Your engineers stay in the loop and keep the tech decisions; we bring the architecture, the GTM logic and the training. It suits teams with existing infrastructure and engineering capacity, and it is the shorter engagement.

**Done For You** designs the full stack - LangGraph, Clay, n8n, PostgreSQL - builds it, deploys it self-hosted in your environment, and hands you ownership. It suits teams who want the system without making a dozen infrastructure calls first.

You can switch models mid-engagement. The methodology does not change, so it costs you nothing to change your mind once the audit tells you more.
