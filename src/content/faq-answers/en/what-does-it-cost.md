**The GTM Audit is ₹15,000** — a written diagnostic delivered in about two to three days, which you keep regardless of what follows.

Build work after that is scoped per engagement, so there is no published rate card. Two variables move it: which engagement model you pick, and how much of the stack already exists. Done With You runs on your infrastructure and tools, which usually narrows the scope. Done For You includes the full design and setup, so it is the larger piece.

Starting with the audit is deliberate. Quoting a build before anyone has looked at the motion is guessing, and you would be paying for the guess.

The intro call before it is free.
