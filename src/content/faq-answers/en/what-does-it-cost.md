**The GTM Audit is $200 to $500** — a written audit delivered in about two to three days, which you keep regardless of what follows. Indian clients are billed the INR equivalent plus GST.

Three things move where you land in that range, and none of them is how long the call was:

- **How tangled the workflow and GTM stack are.** One documented stack with a single source of truth sits at the lower end.
- **How clean and accessible the pipeline data is.** If the history has to be reconstructed before it can be read, that is the work.
- **Whether you have someone technical on your side.** With a data or RevOps counterpart this moves fast. Without one, more of the work and more of the chasing falls to me, and it sits at the upper end.

You are told which applies before anything starts.

**A first paid pilot is $1,200 to $3,000**, scoped from what the audit finds, and moved by the same three factors. One motion, one cohort, one question worth answering. Research and evidence gathering run on the Amoris engine; review, sending and response handling stay human and happen on your infrastructure.

Ongoing build work after that is scoped per engagement, so there is no published rate card. Two variables move it: which engagement model you pick, and how much of the stack already exists. Done With You runs on your infrastructure and tools, which usually narrows the scope. Done For You includes the full design and setup, so it is the larger piece.

Starting with the audit is deliberate. Quoting a build before anyone has looked at the motion is guessing, and you would be paying for the guess.

The intro call before it is free.
