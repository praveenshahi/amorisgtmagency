**The GTM Audit is ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 plus GST** — a written diagnostic delivered in about two to three days, which you keep regardless of what follows.

The range reflects the state of what is being audited, not the length of the conversation. A clean, documented stack with one source of truth sits at the lower end. Several disconnected tools, patchy pipeline data and no clear owner takes materially longer to work through, and sits at the upper end. You are told which end applies before anything starts.

**A first paid pilot runs ₹1.5L to ₹3L plus GST**, scoped from what the audit finds. One motion, one cohort, one question worth answering. Research and evidence gathering run on the Amoris engine; review, sending and response handling stay human and happen on your infrastructure.

Ongoing build work after that is scoped per engagement, so there is no published rate card. Two variables move it: which engagement model you pick, and how much of the stack already exists. Done With You runs on your infrastructure and tools, which usually narrows the scope. Done For You includes the full design and setup, so it is the larger piece.

Starting with the audit is deliberate. Quoting a build before anyone has looked at the motion is guessing, and you would be paying for the guess.

The intro call before it is free.
