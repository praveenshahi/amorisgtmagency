Yes, because the phases run concurrently rather than in sequence.

The audit takes one to two hours. Design runs one to two weeks depending on how much of your stack already exists. Build and deploy take two to four weeks, overlapping with the tail of design.

Six weeks gets you a production system doing the core job. It is not the end of the work - most teams keep iterating for another eight to twelve weeks as real usage data comes in, which is when the sharpest improvements tend to show up.
