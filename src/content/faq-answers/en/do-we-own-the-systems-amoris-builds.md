**Yes, outright.** Systems are deployed on your own infrastructure and you own the workflows, the prompts, the database and the code.

There is no platform to subscribe to, no seat count, and no dependency on Amoris to keep anything running. If you never speak to us again after handover, the system keeps working and your team can change it.

That constraint shapes the build. We document as we go, we avoid anything that would be difficult for your engineers to pick up, and training is part of the engagement rather than an upsell.
