Amoris AI SDR is a research-to-email system. You give it a company name; it researches that company live, analyses the go-to-market fit, forms a commercial hypothesis, writes an evidence-backed outbound email, and then judges its own draft against 14 quality criteria. That whole loop takes about 38 seconds.

Four things make it behave differently from most AI sales tooling:

- **Evidence discipline.** Every claim traces back to a cited source. A hypothesis is framed as curiosity, not as a diagnosis it cannot support.
- **HOLD is a feature.** Most tools always send. This one can decide not to, and tells you why.
- **Signal-first, not template-first.** The argument changes per company, not just the merge field with their name in it.
- **Human in the loop.** It researches and drafts. You send. Judgment stays with you.
