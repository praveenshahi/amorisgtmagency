category: 'general'

**A fit:** Series A or B B2B SaaS companies, roughly 5 to 50 people, with an outbound motion that works but will not scale, no dedicated GTM infrastructure yet, and a willingness to own the system after handover.

**Not a fit:** pre-product companies with no motion to automate, enterprises that need procurement-length timelines, teams shopping for a no-code tool rather than a systems partner, and anyone hoping to outsource judgment entirely.

We would rather say this early than three weeks into a build. If the audit shows we are not the right call, that is a fine outcome.
