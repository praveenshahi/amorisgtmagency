**Amoris is built for teams where outbound still carries someone's name.**

That is a behaviour, not a funding stage or a headcount band. It fits when:

- The founder is still doing their own outbound
- The GTM team is lean, and one person carries both research and messaging
- The sender's own name is on the message, so a bad one costs them something
- The team drafts more than it sends, because quality is the constraint
- Account research and commercial judgement are still done by hand
- The product or service is high-value B2B, where one good conversation justifies the work

**The real exclusion:** teams that want outbound volume regardless of whether the evidence supports the message. A system whose central feature is the ability to stop is the wrong purchase if the goal is more sends.

Also not a fit: anyone wanting autonomous sending (the send is manual by design), or a sequencer, CRM or data enrichment tool.

This is a stated point of view about who the work suits, not a claim validated across a large sample.
